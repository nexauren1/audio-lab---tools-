# Audio Lab Tools — product guide

## Product direction

Audio Lab Tools is an Android-first, bilingual audio toolbox. The product is organized around the user's task instead of asking the user to understand audio engineering before choosing a tool.

## Reference research

The reference guide at https://audio-lab.net/about/ presents its catalogue around five task-oriented groups: Effects, Voice & AI, Edit & convert, Create & analyze, and The lab. Its guides also start with concrete goals, then direct readers to the relevant tool and settings. Audio Lab Tools uses this organizational principle as inspiration, with its own visual system, wording, icon and Android-native navigation.

The Android reference at https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hitrolab.audioeditor further validates the all-in-one toolbox direction: recording, trimming, joining, conversion, mixing and creative tools can sit in one place.

## Information architecture

1. Explore — global search, onboarding hero, category shortcuts and curated previews.
2. Effects — EQ, 8D, reverb, bass boost, speed-based effects and volume control.
3. Voice & AI — vocal tuning, voice transformation, cleanup, transcription and stem-related tools.
4. Edit & convert — trim, join, reverse, pitch, metadata, format conversion and video-to-audio.
5. Create & analyze — beat making, BPM detection, tones, visualizers and soundboard.
6. The lab — experimental time stretching and sound generation.
7. Saved — a personalized shelf of favorite tools.
8. Settings — language choice and project status.

## UX principles

- Global search matches localized tool names and descriptions.
- Every tool is reachable from a category and from search.
- Save/favorite is available directly on each tool card.
- Primary actions are easy to reach on a phone and touch targets are generous.
- Tool workspaces should explain file input, parameters, processing status and output.
- When a tool uses AI, the product must explain whether audio is processed locally or sent to a service, and must not promise perfect source separation, transcription or cloning.
- Voice cloning must require consent and appropriate safeguards.
- The app currently contains the UI foundation and catalogue. DSP/AI processing engines are not connected in this initial scaffold.

## Visual direction

- Near-black ink canvas and deep slate surfaces.
- Mint is the primary action color; category accents distinguish tool families.
- Rounded but restrained cards, thin borders, careful spacing and clear type hierarchy.
- The launcher icon uses a custom audio waveform mark.
- English is the default resource language; Portuguese is available through the in-app language selector.
